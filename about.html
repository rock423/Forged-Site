Our Story

whatshot

Forged In The Heat Of Competition

Forged was born the way all great things are — out of necessity. We were competitive paintball players who lived for the rush of tournament play, the crack of markers, the adrenaline of a breakout. But every match, the same problem: headbands that slipped, soaked through, fell apart, or just looked like afterthoughts. Nothing on the market was built for what we put it through.

So we started making our own. Late nights cutting fabric, testing materials in the Texas heat, running prototypes through weekend scrimmages and national tournaments. We obsessed over the details — moisture-wicking performance, reinforced stitching that wouldn't unravel after fifty washes, designs that carried the same intensity we brought to the field. Word spread through the community. Teammates wanted them. Then rival teams. Then players we'd never even met were reaching out.

That's when we knew this was bigger than us. Forged wasn't just a headband — it was a statement. A mark of the competitor who refuses to settle for generic gear. Every piece we make carries the DNA of real battlefield experience, designed by players who know what it takes to compete at the highest level. This is gear forged for warriors.
